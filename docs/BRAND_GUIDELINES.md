# The Mobile Brake & Rotor Guy — Brand Guidelines

## Core identity

- **Business name:** The Mobile Brake & Rotor Guy
- **Primary tagline:** Professional Brake Service That Comes to You.
- **Positioning:** Mobile brake-focused service for cars, trucks, and vans throughout Southern California’s Inland Empire. Vehicle compatibility is confirmed individually when the customer contacts us.
- **Brand personality:** Professional, friendly, local, transparent, dependable, and safety-focused.

## Color palette

| Token | Hex | Recommended use |
|---|---|---|
| Navy | `#0B2340` | Navigation, headings, primary brand elements |
| Safety Orange | `#F56600` | CTAs, accents, brake caliper, highlights |
| Charcoal | `#1F2933` | Body text and secondary content |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Main backgrounds and negative space |
| Light Gray | `#F1F4F7` | Soft section backgrounds and form surfaces |

## Design rules

- Use navy as the dominant color and orange as the action accent.
- Keep layouts clean, high-contrast, and mobile-first.
- Use bold modern sans-serif typography with generous spacing.
- Avoid racing flames, excessive chrome, aggressive imagery, and clutter.
- Keep call, text, and quote actions visible on mobile.
- Use the logo on white, light gray, or deep navy backgrounds with sufficient contrast.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, or add effects to the logo.

## Website messaging

- **Hero headline:** Brake Service That Comes to You.
- **Primary CTA:** Request a Quote
- **Secondary CTA:** Call or Text
- **Primary services:** Brake pads, rotors, brake fluid service, brake inspections, brake noise and vibration evaluation.
- **Additional services:** Oil changes, oil filters, cabin air filters, and battery replacements when appropriate and available.
- **Vehicle scope:** Cars, trucks, and vans. Do not restrict messaging by country of manufacture or promise support for every make. Review the year, make, model and requested service with each potential customer.
- **Service area:** Southern California Inland Empire; customers should confirm location availability.

## Asset usage

Use the generated PNGs as visual assets for the initial prototype. Before production printing or large-format vehicle graphics, recreate the approved mark as clean vector artwork and confirm final typography and clear-space measurements.
